These signs can point to hail damage. Leave the roof inspection to our crew.

  • Cracked or slipped tiles
  • Missing granules on shingles
  • Dents in vents, gutters, flashing or the AC unit
  • Granules collecting in gutters
  • Blistered foam or flat roof coating
  • New ceiling stains

It doesn't have to look bad to be damaged.

Small dents and bent fins on your AC unit restrict airflow, so it works harder and wears out sooner in the summer heat. A hairline crack in one tile lets water reach the underlayment beneath it. Hail damage starts small and gets worse with every storm.

We inspect your whole roof, including the AC unit, vents and gutters, document every bit of damage and put it all in your insurance claim. Then we handle the claim from start to finish, so you're not chasing it alone.
